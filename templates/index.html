🔬 System Transparency & Known Failure Modes (ESL Bias)

Honest Accuracy Assessment: Statistical detectors analyze sentence length uniformity and academic transition tokens. This instrument explicitly documents failure modes rather than claiming false certainty:

  • ESL False Positives: Non-native English (ESL) writers often use structured transitions (e.g. furthermore, in conclusion) and uniform sentence lengths taught in academic courses, leading to artificial flags.
  • Polished Human Drafts: Heavily edited human essays can mimic machine smoothness.
  • Technical Writing: Highly structured scientific prose naturally exhibits low sentence length variance.
