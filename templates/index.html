System Transparency & Known Failure Modes (ESL Bias)

Statistical analysis measures structural variance and formulaic markers. True explainability requires acknowledging operational boundaries:

ESL Writing Patterns: Non-native English students often utilize structured connectors (e.g., furthermore, in addition), which can inflate machine indicators.
Polished Human Prose: Essays thoroughly revised for clarity may display low sentence variance similar to LLM outputs.
Domain Specificity: Academic or technical prose naturally favors uniform syntax and standardized lexicon.
